# Business Insights, Recommendations and Project Summary

## Final Business Insights
1. The business generated **Rp877.8M in delivered revenue** from **3,000 total orders**, with **787 customers** and an **average order value of Rp494.23K**.
2. **Dress** was the highest-revenue category at **Rp259.7M**.
3. **Jawa Barat** was the highest-revenue province at **Rp169.1M**.
4. Customer retention was strong, with **509 repeat customers**, **278 one-time customers**, and a **64.68% repeat rate**.
5. The **25-34 age group** generated the highest revenue at **Rp372.9M**.
6. **Hadi Fauzi** was the highest-spending customer at approximately **Rp7.8M**.
7. Operations recorded **328 cancelled orders** and **151 returned orders**, with a **10.93% cancellation rate** and **5.03% return rate**.
8. **J&T handled 896 orders**, the largest courier volume. Average shipping cost was **Rp23.42K**, and **Sulawesi Utara** had the highest provincial average shown at approximately **Rp38.33K**.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate cancellation drivers by payment method, category, province, and courier.
2. Review return patterns by product and category to identify recurring issues.
3. Re-engage the **278 one-time customers** to increase repeat purchasing.
4. Focus targeted marketing on the **25-34 age group** while continuing to test other segments.
5. Maintain strong availability and promotion for Dress and other top-performing products.
6. Prioritize Jawa Barat for retention and growth because it generated the highest revenue.
7. Optimize shipping in high-cost provinces such as Sulawesi Utara.
8. Monitor J&T, JNE, and SiCepat closely because they handle most of the order volume.

## Project Summary
**E-Commerce Customer, Order & Operations Analytics** is an end-to-end analytics project built using Python, Pandas, SQL, and Tableau Public.

The project uses five connected e-commerce tables covering customers, orders, order items, products, and reviews. The workflow included data cleaning, missing-value handling, duplicate and data-quality checks, date conversion, relationship validation, exploratory data analysis, customer and order analysis, operational analysis, SQL analysis, and Tableau dashboard development.

Three dashboards were created: **Executive Overview**, **Customer & Product Analysis**, and **Operations & Shipping**. Together, they track revenue, orders, customers, repeat behavior, product performance, age-group performance, geography, cancellations, returns, couriers, and shipping costs.

The project converts raw multi-table data into business KPIs, analytical insights, and practical recommendations.
